NUS Libraries has been conferred a Special Recognition under the IFLA Green Library Award 2026 , administered by the Environment, Sustainability and Libraries Section (ENSULIB) of the International Federation of Library Associations and Institutions (IFLA). The award recognises a library or project that has demonstrated systematic, impactful efforts in advancing sustainability in the library sector and serves as an outstanding example for others.

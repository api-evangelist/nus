Professor Yang Changhuei and Professor Ong Shyue Ping from the College of Design and Engineering (CDE) at NUS have been selected under the prestigious Returning Singaporean Scientists Scheme (RSSS) administered by the National Research Foundation Singapore, for their world-leading expertise in fields that are shaping the future of science and technology. The RSSS initiative is designed to attract top overseas-based Singaporean research leaders back to lead local autonomous universities and research institutes.

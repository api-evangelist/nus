NUS has signed a three-year Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Guidance Tamil Nadu, the Government of Tamil Nadu’s investment promotion agency, to advance cooperation in postgraduate education, talent development and knowledge exchange. The first such agreement between NUS and an Indian state government, the MOU was signed during a two-day series of engagements in Chennai from 28 to 29 August 2026. Spanning industry, government, academia and prospective students, the programme also included an Industry Networking Session and the inaugural NUS Postgraduate Fair.

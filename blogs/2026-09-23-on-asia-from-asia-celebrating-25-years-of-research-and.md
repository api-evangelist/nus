Yoga Prasetyo was only two years old when his mother left their village in Indonesia to work in Singapore. She left him in the care of his uncle and aunt, like many parents whose work took them across borders in search of better opportunities for their families. Years later, Yoga found himself returning to the same story from a different perspective.

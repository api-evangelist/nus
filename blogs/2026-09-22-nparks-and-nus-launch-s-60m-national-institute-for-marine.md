The National Parks Board (NParks) and National University of Singapore (NUS) have launched the National Institute for Marine and Ocean Sciences (NIMOS) , a national research institute that will strengthen Singapore’s capabilities to protect its marine environment and sustainably manage its coastal and marine spaces. The launch ceremony, held today at NUS, was graced by Mr Chee Hong Tat, Minister for National Development, as the Guest-of-Honour.

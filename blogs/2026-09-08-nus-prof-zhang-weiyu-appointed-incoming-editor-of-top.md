Professor Zhang Weiyu from the Department of Communications and New Media at the NUS Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASS) was appointed Co-Chief Editor of the Journal of Communication (JOC) – a leading, internationally renowned academic journal published by Oxford University Press. Established in 1951, JOC is the flagship journal of the International Communication Association based in Washington D.C., and is widely regarded as an essential journal for communication specialists and policymakers alike.

NUS Enterprise celebrated its 25 th anniversary with a new identity: NUSX , where breakthroughs become advantage. The integrated venture-building and research commercialisation platform aims to connect research, talent, industry, capital and global markets to turn discoveries into scalable ventures. Its rebrand was unveiled on 24 September 2026 at the inaugural A5X, Asia's first global innovation and venture summit, which brought together investors, founders, researchers and policymakers.

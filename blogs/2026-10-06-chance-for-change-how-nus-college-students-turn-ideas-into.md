What if a student project wasn’t seen as something merely for experience or grades, but a real chance to change lives? For NUS College (NUSC) students, the answer to these questions came through the experiences they have gained from the College’s flagship Impact Experience programme (IEx). Embodying its philosophy of ‘breaking open the classroom’, IEx allows students to go beyond lectures and books to learn from the communities and environment around the world.

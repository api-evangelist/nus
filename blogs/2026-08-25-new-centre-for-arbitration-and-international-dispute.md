The Ministry of Law (MinLaw) and the National University of Singapore (NUS) will establish a new Centre at the NUS Faculty of Law driving leadership and innovation in arbitration and other forms of international dispute resolution (the “Centre”). Through independent research, thought leadership, international engagement and post-tertiary education, the Centre will reinforce Singapore’s position as a leading global and regional centre for international dispute resolution.

From learning to cycle and cook to forging friendships across cultures, NUS Arts and Social Sciences undergraduate Long Wen Xi reflects on five transformative months at University College Utrecht in the Netherlands. Through NUS’ Student Exchange Programme (SEP), she discovered how embracing the unfamiliar and finding joy in simple moments turned a foreign place into home.
